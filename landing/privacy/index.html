PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated 20 August 2026

The whole policy in one line

Don’t Charge Me Bro collects nothing about you. There is no account, no server behind the app, and no analytics. Everything you type into the app stays on your iPhone. This website is a separate thing from the app, and it gets a section of its own further down.

Who we are

Don’t Charge Me Bro is made by Wellspring Ventures, LLC, and “we” means us throughout this page. Under data protection law such as the UK and EU GDPR we are the data controller for the app — a role with almost nothing in it here, because the app sends us nothing to control.

What the app stores

The app keeps the things you enter so it can show them back to you and remind you before a charge lands:

  • The subscriptions and free trials you add — name, amount, billing cycle, and the date of the next charge.
  • Charges you let through, which build your Hall of Shame total.
  • Your settings and progress — roast level, win streak, stickers earned, and the total you have saved.

All of it is written to your device’s own app storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere. We cannot see it, because there is nowhere for it to arrive.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address, no password.
  • No card details. The app never asks for, sees, or stores payment information — it does not connect to your bank, your card, or your App Store account.
  • No servers of ours. The iPhone app makes no network requests at all — there is nothing to send and nowhere to send it. The website you are reading is separate, and is described in This website below.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party trackers, no advertising identifier.
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera access.

Notifications

If you turn on roasts, the app asks iOS for permission to send notifications and then schedules them locally on your device. There is no push server. The reminder text, including the subscription name and amount, is composed on your iPhone and handed to iOS, which delivers it at the scheduled time. If you deny or later revoke notification permission in iOS Settings, the app keeps working — it simply stops reminding you.

Sharing

When you tap share on a win or a receipt, the app makes an image on your device and passes it to the standard iOS share sheet. What happens next is entirely your choice and is governed by whichever app you send it to. Nothing is shared unless you send it.

Your purchase

Don’t Charge Me Bro costs $1.99 once. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see your payment details. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated sales and download reports that cannot identify you. Apple’s handling of that transaction is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Ratings prompt

After a few wins the app may ask iOS to show the standard App Store review prompt. That prompt is displayed by Apple; we do not receive your rating or review through the app, and the app records only that it has already asked, so it never asks twice.

This website

Everything above is about the iPhone app. These pages are not the app, and they are served the way any website is — so this is what happens when you visit one.

We run two things on the web. dontchargeme.app, including this page, is a set of static pages hosted by Cloudflare. app.dontchargeme.app is a version of Don’t Charge Me Bro that runs in a browser, hosted by Railway. Both hosts keep the ordinary request logs every web server keeps — your IP address, your browser’s user agent, and which page you asked for — which is what lets them serve the page and block abuse. We do not use those logs to build a profile of you, we do not combine them with anything else, and we cannot connect them to anything in the app, because the app sends us nothing to connect them to.

What these pages do not do: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no third-party embeds. The fonts and images are served from our own domains rather than from Google or a CDN, precisely so that loading a page about privacy does not hand your IP address to somebody else.

If you use the browser version at app.dontchargeme.app, your subscription list is stored by your own browser and never sent to us. The server sends the page; your list stays on your machine, and clearing that site’s data in your browser deletes it. It is a convenience for trying the thing out on a desktop — the iPhone app is the real one, and only the iPhone app can schedule reminders that reach you when the browser is closed.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights, and how to exercise them

Rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability under laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA all assume a company holds data about you. We hold none. You already have direct control: edit or remove any entry inside the app, and delete every last byte by deleting the app from your iPhone. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change with it.
