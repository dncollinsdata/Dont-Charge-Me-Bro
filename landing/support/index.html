A reminder didn’t arrive

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS, so when one goes missing it is nearly always one of these:

  1. Notification permission. Open the ROASTS tab. If it shows an ALLOW button, the app has not been given permission yet — tap it. If it says notifications are blocked, iOS needs to grant it back: Settings → Don’t Charge Me Bro → Notifications.
  2. Focus or Do Not Disturb. The reminders in the final stretch — the day before, the morning of, and the 8pm last call — are sent as Time Sensitive so they can break through Focus, but only if you let them: Settings → Don’t Charge Me Bro → Notifications → Time Sensitive Notifications.
  3. Too many subscriptions queued. iOS only lets an app hold a limited number of pending notifications. If you track a lot of them, the app fills that queue and reminds you to reopen it so it can schedule the next batch. If you get that nudge, open the app — that is all it needs.
  4. The date was wrong. The app only knows the renewal date you typed in. If the merchant bills on a different day, the roast lands on the wrong one.

Still nothing? Email us with your iOS version and roughly how many subscriptions you track, and we’ll dig in.

The roasts are too much (or not enough)

ROASTS tab → ROAST LEVEL. Mild is a straight heads-up, medium is mildly disappointed in you, and UNHINGED is what it sounds like. It changes every reminder from then on.

Removing something, or fixing a mistake

Tap YEET on any row to remove it. There is no separate edit — if you typed the wrong amount or date, yeet it and add it again.

One thing worth knowing: yeeting counts as a win, so removing a typo will nudge your savings total and streak up. Those numbers exist to keep you motivated, not to audit you.

Can the app cancel a subscription for me?

No, and it never will. Cancelling means acting on your account with the company that bills you — the app has no access to that, or to your card, or to your App Store billing. It gets you to the deadline with time to spare; the cancelling is yours to do.

For subscriptions billed through Apple, the fastest route is Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions.

I got a new phone / I deleted the app

Your list lives only on your device — there is no account and no server, which is the trade for us never seeing your data. It also means there is no backup we can restore from, and no way to move a list between phones. Deleting the app deletes everything in it.

Refunds

Apple handles all billing for the app, so refunds go through them at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple turns you down and you think that’s wrong, email us and we’ll see what we can do.

Privacy, in one line

The app collects nothing. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. The long version is on the privacy page.
